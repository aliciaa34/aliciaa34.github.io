Presented as an oral contribution at SPIE Astronomical Telescopes + Instrumentation 2026 in
Copenhagen. The proceedings paper is in press.

HARPS3 is being built for the Terra Hunting Experiment, and reaching 10 cm s<sup>-1</sup>
radial-velocity precision requires detailed knowledge of detector behaviour. Uniform
flat-field frames are essential for identifying non-uniform pixels and surface defects that
would otherwise imprint spurious features on the extracted spectra.

This paper presents the HARPS3 flat-field light source: a ring of white LEDs mounted between
the camera objective and the CCD. White LEDs were chosen to better represent the full optical
range, and diffusion rings lower the flux and improve the uniformity of the illumination.
