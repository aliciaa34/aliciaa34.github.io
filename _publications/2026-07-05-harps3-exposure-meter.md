Presented as a poster at SPIE Astronomical Telescopes + Instrumentation 2026 in Copenhagen.
The proceedings paper is in press.

The design and verification of the HARPS3 exposure meter, a dual-band photometer that
monitors the light reaching the spectrograph throughout an exposure. Weighting the exposure
by the photons actually collected gives a more accurate effective midpoint than the nominal
one, which in turn improves the barycentric correction applied to the measured radial
velocities.
