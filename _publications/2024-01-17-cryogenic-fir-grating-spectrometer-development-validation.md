This paper presents the design, integration and laboratory validation of a 4 K diffraction grating spectrometer operating from 285 to 500 um, used to post-disperse the output of a room-temperature polarizing Fourier transform spectrometer.

Diffraction efficiency and resolving power were measured as functions of wavelength and polarization and shown to agree with theoretical models. These are the first measurements of blazed grating efficiency at cryogenic temperatures in the far-infrared, and they establish the feasibility of hybrid post-dispersed polarizing FTS instruments for spaceborne far-infrared observatories such as SPICA-SAFARI and PRIMA.
