Astrophysics PhD candidate at the University of Cambridge specialising in high-resolution
spectroscopy and astronomical instrumentation. My research focuses on mitigating instrumental
and pipeline systematics in extreme-precision radial velocity measurements with HARPS3, the
high-resolution cross-dispersed echelle spectrograph built for the Terra Hunting Experiment.
I have experience in cryogenic instrumentation for space- and ground-based applications,
optical design and assembly, and the development of custom analysis and simulation tools for
spectroscopy.

Research experience
======

**University of Cambridge** &mdash; PhD in Astrophysics, Cambridge, UK &middot; *October 2022 &ndash; present*
* Participated in integration and verification tasks for HARPS3, a high-resolution cross-dispersed spectrograph designed for extreme-precision radial velocity measurements.
* Led the adaptation and validation of the HARPS3 data reduction pipeline, with a focus on optimisation and minimising systematics.
* Contributed to the assembly, integration and verification of HARPS3 for first-light observations at the Isaac Newton Telescope in La Palma, Spain.

**University of Cambridge** &mdash; Research Assistantship, Cambridge, UK &middot; *November 2021*
* Executed Energy Absorption Interferometry (EAI) experiments using a near-infrared InGaAs camera.
* Probed and characterised the absorption modes of an InGaAs camera, extending the theory from a single absorber to an array of pixels.

**University of Lethbridge** &mdash; Research Internship, Lethbridge, Canada &middot; *May &ndash; September 2022*
* Implemented a novel rear-mounted mirror calibration method to directly measure diffraction grating efficiency in situ.
* Integrated a cryogenic tunable THz photomixer line source with the grating and bolometer detector, enabling the first fully cryogenic performance verification.
* Collected and analysed experimental data for system characterisation and contributed to peer-reviewed publications.

**University of Lethbridge** &mdash; MSc in Physics, Lethbridge, Canada &middot; *May 2020 &ndash; May 2022*
* Responsible for the design, integration and validation of a grating spectrometer module, and for integrating it with other modules for full end-to-end verification.
* Characterised grating performance: measured diffraction efficiency and resolving power as functions of wavelength and polarisation, achieving agreement with theoretical models.
* Results represent the first measurements of blazed diffraction grating efficiency at cryogenic temperatures in the far-infrared, demonstrating the feasibility of hybrid PDPFTS instruments for future spaceborne FIR observatories such as SPICA-SAFARI and PRIMA.

**Blue Sky Spectroscopy Inc.** &mdash; New Technologies for Canadian Observatories Internship, Lethbridge, Canada &middot; *May 2020 &ndash; May 2022*
* Characterised the temperature response of an InAs infrared detector from &minus;40&nbsp;&deg;C to +20&nbsp;&deg;C using a high-resolution FTIR spectrometer; analysed detector cutoff shift via normalised spectra.
* Used an InGaAs detector to measure the efficiency of an integrating sphere as a function of temperature from 300&nbsp;K to 4&nbsp;K for applications in cryogenic laser metrology.
* Conducted FTIR transmission spectroscopy of polypropylene-coated PPE mask materials and developed a quantitative absorption-based metric to rank material quality.

**Blue Sky Spectroscopy Inc.** &mdash; Research Internship, Lethbridge, Canada &middot; *September 2019 &ndash; April 2020*
* Researched and benchmarked low-cost thermal vacuum (TVAC) chamber designs to simulate space-like conditions for component testing.
* Compared thermal, structural and vacuum properties across published designs, evaluating cooling systems, radiative shielding and material performance to inform the design of a custom TVAC chamber.
* Performed thermal and structural FEA of a cryogenic grating enclosure using ANSYS Workbench, simulating contraction and strain from 300&nbsp;K to 4&nbsp;K.

**University of Lethbridge** &mdash; NSERC Undergraduate Student Research Award, Lethbridge, Canada &middot; *May &ndash; August 2019*
* Specification and modelling of a custom diffraction grating for use in series with a high-resolution Fourier transform spectrometer for far-infrared spectroscopy.
* Used SolidWorks to design a CAD model for a monolithic enclosure for the grating spectrometer to ensure optical alignment stability at 4&nbsp;K.
* Conducted optical and mechanical modelling and an FEA analysis to validate alignment tolerances and integration of the grating spectrometer into a test facility cryostat.

**University of Calgary** &mdash; NSERC Undergraduate Student Research Award, Calgary, Canada &middot; *May &ndash; August 2018*
* Operated a pulsed supersonic jet expansion apparatus with cryogenic vacuum systems to obtain spectra of extremely cold (sub-K) molecular clusters using quantum cascade and optical parametric oscillator lasers.
* Designed and implemented a saturated absorption experiment to achieve extremely narrow Doppler-free absorption line profiles, testing and verifying the resolution of an optical parametric oscillator laser.

**University of Lethbridge** &mdash; NSERC Undergraduate Student Research Award, Lethbridge, Canada &middot; *May &ndash; August 2017*
* Used numerical simulations and galaxy models to assess the feasibility of a Fourier transform spectrometer for astronomical applications.
* Used Zemax OpticStudio to model the optical design of a scannable Fabry&ndash;P&eacute;rot interferometer for infrared spectroscopy.
