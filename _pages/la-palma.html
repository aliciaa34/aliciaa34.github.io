Daylight and dusk at Roque de los Muchachos, 2400 m up on La Palma, where HARPS3 sits on the Isaac Newton Telescope. Most of the weather happens below the ridge, so the observing nights tend to start with the sun going down into a sea of cloud. Scroll or swipe through, or see them all at once. The night-sky pictures are on the Astrophotography page.
